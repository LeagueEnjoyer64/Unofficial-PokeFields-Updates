# Thank you for playing

This is the final update to the Unofficial PokeFields APK.

This app was made so trainers without a PC could enjoy PokeFields. Now that the official Android app is available, the unofficial build has served its purpose.

The Unofficial PokeFields APK will no longer receive updates. Please move to the official app for future updates.

Thank you to everyone who downloaded it, tested it, reported bugs, and played along. Thank you for using it and being part of the journey.

[Download official PokeFields for Android](https://update.pokefields.com/download?platform=android)
